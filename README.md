## 🌐 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global **Configure, Price, Quote (CPQ)** software market is valued at approximately **$3.6B to $3.9B in 2026** (projected to reach over **$5.8B by 2028** at a **CAGR of ~16–18%**). The sector is **moderately concentrated**—dominated at the top end by mega-cap enterprise CRM/ERP suites (Salesforce, Oracle, SAP), yet balanced by specialized, high-growth mid-market and manufacturing solutions (DealHub, Conga, Tacton, PandaDoc) that thrive where vertical-specific product rules, visual 3D configuration, or agile usage-based billing prevent a single winner-take-all monopoly.

# Awesome-Configure-Price-Quote-CPQ
**Top Configure Price Quote CPQ (Opensource) 🌟 Star if you like it! 🌟**

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Product Configuration, Guided Selling, Quote-to-Cash Automation & Self-Hosted Revenue Platforms*
**Last updated: November 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Configure, Price, Quote (CPQ)**. These tools enable sales teams to configure complex products, calculate accurate pricing, and generate professional quotes—from fully managed enterprise CPQ suites with AI-driven guided selling to open-source modules that embed CPQ logic into broader CRM/ERP frameworks.

**Examples** include Salesforce CPQ, HubSpot Pricing and Quotes, DealHub, PandaDoc, Conga CPQ, PROS CPQ, Cacheflow, Subskribe, Kugamon, and Nuance CPQ (the category leaders in commercial space).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with every relevant project for self-hosted CPQ, product configuration, and open quote-to-cash infrastructure. **Critical note**: The open-source CPQ landscape is nascent and fragmented—**Dainamite CPQ** is the most complete purpose-built module, but it requires the **Open Mercato** CRM/ERP framework as its foundation . **SwiftCPQ** offers a focused proposal tool for managed service providers , and **BillaBear** provides billing with basic quote generation but not true product configuration . No open-source project matches Salesforce CPQ's depth of product rules, bundle configuration, and approval workflows out of the box.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce CPQ](https://www.salesforce.com/)**
  **Enterprise CPQ** (formerly Steelbrick, acquired by Salesforce in 2016)  with product rules, bundle configuration, pricing engines, and approval workflows. **Pricing**: Quote-only; per-user licensing. **Free tier**: None. **Best for**: Salesforce customers needing deep CPQ within their CRM—**trade-off**: implementation complexity and consulting costs.

- **[HubSpot Pricing and Quotes](https://www.hubspot.com/)**
  **CPQ and e-signature** within HubSpot Sales Hub. Professional ($85/seat/month) adds quotes, tiered pricing, and automated sales tax; Enterprise adds advanced approvals . **Pricing**: Per-seat; quote-based for enterprise. **Free tier**: Limited basic invoices. **Best for**: HubSpot CRM users wanting quote-to-cash without leaving the platform—**trade-off**: not a full product configurator for complex BOMs.

- **[DealHub](https://www.dealhub.io/)**
  **CPQ and deal desk platform** with interactive buyer dealrooms and AI-assisted quoting. **Pricing**: Quote-only. **Free tier**: None. **Best for**: revenue teams wanting guided selling with buyer-facing dealrooms.

- **[PandaDoc](https://www.pandadoc.com/)**
  **Document automation** with CPQ and e-signature capabilities. **Pricing**: Tiered per-user; quote-based for enterprise. **Free tier**: Trial available. **Best for**: teams wanting quotes and contracts in one document workflow.

- **[Conga CPQ](https://conga.com/)**
  **Enterprise CPQ** within Conga's revenue lifecycle management suite. **Pricing**: Quote-only. **Free tier**: None. **Best for**: enterprises needing CPQ integrated with contract management and billing.

- **[PROS CPQ](https://www.pros.com/)**
  **AI-driven CPQ** with dynamic pricing and guided selling. **Pricing**: Quote-only. **Free tier**: None. **Best for**: B2B enterprises needing AI-powered price optimization alongside quoting.

- **[Cacheflow](https://www.cacheflow.com/)**
  **CPQ and billing** for SaaS and subscription businesses. **Pricing**: Quote-only. **Free tier**: None. **Best for**: SaaS companies wanting quote-to-cash with subscription billing.

- **[Subskribe](https://www.subskribe.com/)**
  **CPQ and subscription management** for SaaS. **Pricing**: Quote-only. **Free tier**: None. **Best for**: SaaS businesses needing unified quoting and subscription lifecycle management.

- **[Kugamon](https://www.kugamon.com/)**
  **CPQ and billing** for Salesforce and NetSuite. **Pricing**: Quote-only. **Free tier**: Trial available. **Best for**: mid-market companies wanting CPQ that bridges CRM and ERP.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Dainamite CPQ (@dainamite/cpq)](https://socket.dev/npm/package/@dainamite/cpq)** 
  **Configure, Price, Quote engine for Open Mercato** (license per package) — **the most feature-complete open-source CPQ module** found in this research, built as an extension to the **Open Mercato** CRM/ERP framework . **Features**: **Configurable product attributes**; **multi-charge pricing tables** (one-time, monthly, usage-based); **guided quoting wizards**; **customer inventory and subscription tracking**; **ARC (Amend/Renew/Cancel) flows**; **quote → order → activation pipeline**; **product bundling**; **pricing rules and configuration wizards** . **Required Open Mercato modules**: auth, directory, catalog, sales, customers, dictionaries . **Tech stack**: TypeScript (Next.js/Open Mercato). **Deploy**: Self-hosted (`yarn add @dainamite/cpq`, register in Open Mercato host app). **Trade-off**: **Requires Open Mercato framework**—not a standalone CPQ product. The CPQ module is one part of a broader CRM/ERP foundation . Early-stage (v0.4.0) with limited independent validation. **Best for**: developers building on Open Mercato wanting a structured CPQ module with product catalog, pricing tables, quoting, and subscription lifecycle—without building CPQ logic from scratch.

- **[SwiftCPQ](https://github.com/topics/configure-price-quote)** 
  **Open-source, vendor-agnostic proposal tool** (Vue, license per repo) — **the most focused standalone open-source CPQ alternative for managed service providers and IT solution providers**. **Features**: Positioned as an accessible alternative to proprietary CPQ tools like ConnectWise CPQ, Kaseya Quote Manager, and Scalepad Quoter . **Tech stack**: Vue. **Deploy**: Self-hosted. **Trade-off**: **Proposal-focused**—less about complex product configuration and more about generating quotes/proposals. Minimal documentation available in search results. **Best for**: MSPs and IT providers wanting a free alternative to commercial CPQ quoting tools—**evaluate feature completeness against your specific quoting needs**.

- **[openCPQ](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/opencpq-matthews@0.2.7/README.md)** 
  **Library for in-browser product configuration** (license per package) — **the most developer-friendly open-source product configurator library**. **Features**: In-browser product configuration; example configurators for optical transport products and component demos; designed as a library to embed in web applications . **Tech stack**: JavaScript. **Deploy**: npm library. **Trade-off**: **Configuration only**—no pricing engine, quoting workflow, or CRM integration. It is a UI library, not a CPQ platform. **Best for**: developers wanting to embed product configuration logic into custom applications—especially where the configurator is one component of a larger system.

- **[BillaBear](https://github.com/billabear/billabear)** 
  **Self-hostable billing system with quotes** (license per repo) — **a billing platform that includes basic quote generation** alongside subscriptions and invoicing. **Features**: Quotes, subscriptions, invoices, workflows, tax management, multi-currency, Stripe integration, and a feature comparison against Lago, Stripe, ChargeBee, and Recurly . **Tech stack**: PHP (Symfony). **Deploy**: Self-hosted. **Trade-off**: **Billing-first, not CPQ**—quotes are simple line-item documents, not configurable product bundles with rules and guided selling. No product configuration engine. **Best for**: subscription businesses wanting self-hosted billing with basic quoting—**not suitable for complex CPQ requirements**.

- **[SolidInvoice](https://github.com/solidinvoice/solidinvoice)** 
  **Open-source invoicing and quoting solution** (MIT) — **the most mature open-source billing tool with quote-to-invoice workflow**. **Features**: Quotes that convert to invoices in one click; recurring invoices; multi-currency; multi-tax support; 8 PDF templates; REST API with MCP server for AI agents; self-hosted or hosted ($8/month) . **Tech stack**: Symfony 7, PHP 8.4, API Platform 4. **Deploy**: Docker, single binary, or hosted. **Trade-off**: **Invoicing-first**—no product configuration, pricing rules, or guided selling. Quotes are document generation, not CPQ. **Best for**: small businesses and freelancers wanting self-hosted invoicing with simple quotes—**not for enterprise CPQ**.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Odoo Product Configurator (pledra/odoo-product-configurator)** — Odoo modules enabling dynamic product configuration (Python, ~104 stars) . **Note**: **Odoo-dependent**—requires Odoo ERP; configuration module only, not full CPQ. **Best for**: Odoo users wanting product configuration within their ERP.
- **DestinX Product Configurator for WooCommerce** — Free WooCommerce product configurator (GPL) . **Note**: **E-commerce product configurator**, not B2B CPQ—no quoting, approval workflows, or CRM integration. **Best for**: WooCommerce store owners wanting guided product configuration at checkout.
- **Product Configurator for WooCommerce** — Open-source WooCommerce configurator with 3D support (GPL) . **Note**: Similar to DestinX—e-commerce focus, not sales CPQ. **Best for**: WooCommerce stores needing visual product configuration.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Dainamite CPQ** on Open Mercato for the most complete open-source CPQ foundation with **SwiftCPQ** for focused proposal generation and **BillaBear** or **SolidInvoice** for billing/quote document generation—creating a CPQ-adjacent stack. **None of these match Salesforce CPQ's product rules, bundle configuration, and approval workflows**; those require commercial platforms or significant custom development on Open Mercato.

- **The "full CPQ platform" vs. "CPQ module" vs. "proposal tool" vs. "billing with quotes" distinction is critical.** **Salesforce CPQ, HubSpot Pricing and Quotes, DealHub, Conga CPQ, and PROS CPQ** are **full CPQ platforms**—they handle product configuration, pricing rules, guided selling, and approval workflows. **Dainamite CPQ** is a **CPQ module** within Open Mercato—it provides configurable products, pricing tables, and quoting, but requires the broader framework . **SwiftCPQ** is a **proposal tool**—focused on generating quotes/proposals, not complex product configuration . **BillaBear and SolidInvoice** are **billing platforms with quotes**—they generate quote documents but lack product configuration and CPQ logic . **openCPQ** is a **configuration library**—it handles the configurator UI, not pricing or quoting . **Choose based on whether you need full CPQ, a module to build upon, or just quote document generation.**
- **Open-source CPQ is immature.** **Dainamite CPQ** is the most complete option, but it is **v0.4.0** and depends entirely on **Open Mercato** . **SwiftCPQ** has minimal public documentation . **If you need production-grade CPQ with product rules, bundle configuration, and approval workflows today, a commercial platform or significant custom development is required.**
- **Dainamite CPQ's strength is its integration with Open Mercato's broader CRM/ERP foundation**—it is not a standalone product but a revenue management module within a larger framework . **The trade-off**: you must adopt Open Mercato to use it. **For teams building on Open Mercato, it provides a structured path to CPQ without starting from scratch.**
- **BillaBear and SolidInvoice are not CPQ tools.** They generate quotes as documents—BillaBear for billing , SolidInvoice for invoicing . **Neither provides product configuration, pricing rules, or guided selling.** Do not evaluate them for CPQ requirements.
- **Pricing opacity is universal across commercial CPQ vendors.** Salesforce, HubSpot (enterprise), DealHub, PandaDoc (enterprise), Conga, PROS, Cacheflow, Subskribe, and Kugamon all require sales contact for quotes. **Validate with direct vendor conversations against your product complexity and sales cycle.**
- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper infrastructure (Open Mercato for Dainamite CPQ, PHP/Symfony for BillaBear/SolidInvoice), security hardening for pricing data, and ongoing maintenance. **CPQ systems handle negotiated pricing and customer commitments**—ensure your deployment meets your organization's security and compliance requirements before processing live quotes.
